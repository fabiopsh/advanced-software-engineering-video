## Pagina 16

How to get the credits
Ordinary exam
The ordinary exam consists of two parts:
(1) A hands-on pass/fail test on the technologies used in the lab, followed by
(2) An oral interview on the topics of syllabus (theory + lab)


## Pagina 17 — 1 immagini/figure: guardare il PNG

How to get the credits (cont.)
Continuous assessment (optional)
The continuous assessment will consist of 
(A) two in-class MCQ tests during the course, and of 
(B) a team lab project to be delivered before the end of the course
The in-class MCQ tests will be considered passed with an average of at least 18/30.
The team lab project will be considered passed after passing an oral interview on the 
project.

## Pagina 18

How to get the credits (cont.)
FAQ
Q: What will the “hands-on pass/fail test on the technologies used in the lab” consist of? 
A: It will consist of performing some tasks by using some of the tools used in the lab 
sessions. 
Q: If I pass the hands-on lab test but I do not positively conclude the exam in the same 
exam date, do I have to take the lab test again? 
A: No, a passed lab test will remain valid till the end of 2027.
Q: Which is the “weight” of the theory and lab part in the final evaluation? 
A: 2/3 theory and 1/3 lab
Q: If I pass the MCQ tests in the “continuous assessment” and decide to take the 
(optional) oral interview on theory, my final evaluation will be an average of the MCQ 
tests and of the oral interview?
A: Yes


## Pagina 19

How to get the credits (cont.)
Q: Can I participate in MCQ#2 even if my evaluation in MCQ#1 was less than 18/30?
A: Yes
Q: Can I pass the MCQs even if my evaluation in MCQ#1 was less than 18/30?
A: Yes, if the average of your two MCQs will be at least 18/30 
Q: What happens if finally I will not pass the MCQs?
A: You can either (a) take the ordinary exam or (b) do the lab project as continuous 
assessment and take an oral interview only on the theory part
Q: The exam rules state that if I will pass the MCQs, I can ask “to take an oral interview 
on theory”. What is that?
A: If you pass the MCQs and you would like to try improving your evaluation on the 
theory part, you can ask to take an oral interview on the topics of the MCQ in which you 
got the lower score. The evaluation of the oral interview will replace the evaluation of 
such MCQ. Here are some examples:
