## Pagina 62

Experiments
• We experimented 3 (x 3) policies
• Uniform: partition shots uniformly across all available QPUs
• Two noise-aware policies 
• Calibration phase – executing a training set of circuits on each QPU to estimate 
noise by comparing results with executing on exact simulator – to estimate the 
reliability of each target QPU
• Hellinger: partition shots to minimize the total (squared) Hellinger distance of 
expected QPU results 
• MISE: partition shots taking into account also expected statistical errors 
(minimum Mean Integrated Square Error)
• Experiments run on 7 QPUs from IBM and IonQ

Remarks
Challenges
• shot-wise distribution may dilute best-case performance (of best QPU)
• noise-aware policies depend on accuracy and frequency of QPU calibrations
Overall conclusions: shot-wise distribution is worth it!
Extensions
• investigate further shot-wise error mitigation
• consider other split&merge policies
• develop tailored calibrations for specific scenarios
• study incremental executions (to reduce number of shots)
see later
